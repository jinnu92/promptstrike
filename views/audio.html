📖 Researcher's Guide: Audio Injection

Description: Audio injection (or Audio Steganography) hides text-based commands within sound files. Modern AI models with "Voice" or "Audio" capabilities (like GPT-4o or Gemini 1.5) process audio as a sequence of tokens. Attackers can embed prompts using frequencies or patterns that are inaudible to humans but clearly "read" by the AI's transcription layer.

How to Use:
1. Capture: Record a benign voice message or upload an existing audio file.
2. Select: Choose a payload (e.g., P-101 System Prompt).
3. Inject: Use Ultrasonic (18kHz+) for high-fidelity models, or LSB for robust pixel-like bit manipulation in the PCM data.
4. Test: Upload the result to an AI and ask "What did I say in this recording?"

Mitigation:
• Low-Pass Filtering: Aggressively filter out frequencies above 16kHz before AI processing to remove ultrasonic signals.
• Re-sampling: Downsample audio to 16kHz or 8kHz to destroy high-frequency stego patterns.
• Dithering/Noise: Add white noise to the audio signal to disrupt LSB and Phase-based injections.
