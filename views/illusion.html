📖 Researcher's Guide: Hybrid Frequency Attacks

Description: This attack exploits the Spatial Frequency Gap between human vision and AI vision backbones (like CLIP/ViT). Humans prioritize high-frequency components (edges), while AI models rely on low-frequency global structures (blobs) for classification.

How to Test:
1. Upload two different people or objects.
2. Use the Distance Slider. Notice how Person A disappears and Person B emerges at high blur levels.
3. Point a camera at the "Human Perception" preview. The AI will often identify the subject as the target in the "Model Perception" box.

Mitigation: Implement multi-scale feature verification. Vision models should be trained to detect frequency mismatches and flag hybrid images as anomalous.
