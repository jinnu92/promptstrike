📖 Researcher's Guide: Multi-Turn Attacks

What are Multi-Turn Attacks?
Multi-turn attacks exploit the conversational nature of LLMs by spreading a malicious objective across multiple messages. Each individual message may appear harmless, but the cumulative effect gradually steers the model past its safety boundaries.

Key Techniques:

  • Warm-Up: Begin with innocent questions to establish a cooperative pattern.
  • Trust Building: Claim authority or expertise to lower the model's guard.
  • Context Flooding: Fill the context window with benign text to push safety instructions out of the attention window.
  • Persona Shift: Use fictional scenarios to blur the boundary between roleplay and reality.
  • Compliance Fatigue: Make many benign requests, then slip in a harmful one.
How to Test:
  1. Select a sequence and copy each turn one by one into the target model.
  2. Paste the model's response into the response box for each turn.
  3. Observe at which turn the model begins to comply or leak information.
  4. Export the full session as JSON or Markdown for documentation.
